Marketplace Standard Commission Structure

ShopSense Marketplace charges a tiered commission on standard retail sales. High volume sales receive optimized rates.

Commission Model:
Standard Flat Tiered (10% standard rate, dropping to 7.5% after ₹10,000 monthly sales volume)
Fulfillment Term:
Vendor agrees to fulfill and ship products within 48 business hours of order placement.
Payment Terms:
Payouts are generated weekly via direct bank transfer (ACH) for cleared sales.
